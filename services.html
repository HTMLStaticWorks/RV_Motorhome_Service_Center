01 / PREVENTIVE CARE

Routine Maintenance & 50-Point Audits

Prevent roadside breakdowns before they disrupt your itinerary. We provide thorough multi-point vehicle inspections for motorized and towable RVs, ensuring every critical component from chassis to rooftop meets factory specifications.

  • Chassis & Powertrain Service: Synthetic fluid flushes, oil & filter replacement, brake pads, and heavy-duty chassis greasing.
  • Wheel Bearings & Suspension: Complete bearing clean, inspection, high-temp repack, seal replacement, and electric brake adjustment.
  • Cummins Onan Generator Service: Spark plugs, fuel filter, oil flush, valve lash check, and full electrical load bank testing.

03 / SEASONAL PROTECTION

All-Weather Winterization & Spring Prep

Protect your coach from costly freeze damage. Our master technicians inject non-toxic propylene glycol antifreeze into all plumbing lines, blow out residual moisture with regulated compressed air, and prepare house batteries for sub-zero temperatures.

  • Water Heater Bypass: Isolating and draining the water heater tank and removing sacrificial anode rods.
  • Propylene Glycol Injection: Food-grade RV plumbing antifreeze flushed through all faucets, showers, toilets, and city inlets.
  • Spring De-Winterization Prep: Complete system sanitization, fresh water tank flushing, and chlorine neutralization.
